## One-line summary

Review workloads against the six pillars and record risks, improvements, and milestones.

## Core capabilities and use cases

- Reviews operational excellence, security, reliability, performance efficiency, cost optimization, and sustainability.
- Records high- and medium-risk issues, improvement guidance, and milestones.
- Supports repeated reviews so teams can compare architecture improvements over time.
- The team defines a workload and answers framework questions using real architectural context.
- It is a structured review tool, not an automatic architecture redesign or remediation engine.

## Exam focus and common pitfalls

- Six pillars, workload review, risk issues, and milestones point to the Well-Architected Tool.
- Trusted Advisor performs automated account checks; the Well-Architected Tool guides a contextual workload review.
- The review quality depends on accurate and complete input.

## Key takeaway

**Six pillars plus workload review, risks, and milestones means AWS Well-Architected Tool.**
