## One-line summary

Retrieve AWS compliance reports on demand and review or manage applicable agreements.

## Core capabilities and use cases

- Artifact Reports provides third-party audit and compliance documents such as SOC, ISO, and PCI reports.
- Artifact Agreements lets authorized teams review, accept, and manage applicable agreements.
- It supplies AWS-side evidence for audits, risk assessments, and vendor due diligence.
- Access to reports and agreements is controlled through account permissions.
- It does not scan customer resources or prove that a customer workload is compliant.

## Exam focus and common pitfalls

- Choose Artifact when an auditor requests an AWS SOC, ISO, or PCI report or an agreement.
- Audit Manager organizes evidence from the customer environment; Config evaluates resource configuration.
- AWS compliance reports do not remove the customer's shared-responsibility obligations.

## Key takeaway

**Use Artifact for AWS reports and agreements; use Audit Manager for customer-environment evidence.**
