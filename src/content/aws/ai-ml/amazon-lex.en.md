## In one sentence

A managed service for text and voice conversational interfaces, chatbots, and contact-center bots.

## Key points

- It uses intents, slots, and dialog flows to understand requests and collect required information.
- It commonly invokes Lambda for business logic and integrates with Amazon Connect.
- Lex manages conversation; Polly is text to speech, and Transcribe is speech to text.

## Exam focus

- Chatbots, voice bots, intents, and slots point to Amazon Lex.

## Common pitfalls

- Do not choose by product name alone; confirm data type, latency, control, operations, and cost constraints.

## Remember

**Lex handles the conversation; Lambda performs the business action.**
