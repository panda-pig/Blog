## In one sentence

A managed text-to-speech service that turns text into natural-sounding speech.

## Key points

- It fits voice announcements, audio content, accessibility, and voice applications.
- You can control language, voice, and speech behavior; text goes in and audio comes out.
- Do not confuse it with Amazon Transcribe, which converts speech to text.

## Exam focus

- Choose Polly for text to speech and narration.

## Common pitfalls

- Do not choose by product name alone; confirm data type, latency, control, operations, and cost constraints.

## Remember

**Polly speaks; Transcribe writes.**
