## In one sentence

A managed service that creates personalized recommendations and ranking from users, items, and interactions.

## Key points

- It fits product recommendations, content recommendations, similar items, and personalized ranking.
- It needs high-quality interaction data and evaluation of cold start, bias, and business metrics.
- Compare SageMaker when features, algorithms, and training workflows must be fully customized.

## Exam focus

- Personalized recommendations and user-item interactions point to Personalize.

## Common pitfalls

- Do not choose by product name alone; confirm data type, latency, control, operations, and cost constraints.

## Remember

**Managed recommendations use Personalize; fully custom models use SageMaker.**
