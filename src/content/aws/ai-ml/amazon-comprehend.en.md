## In one sentence

Use natural-language processing to identify sentiment, entities, key phrases, language, and topics in text.

## Key points

- It fits analysis of feedback, tickets, reviews, and documents.
- The input must already be text; transcribe audio first with Transcribe.
- Comprehend understands text; it does not generate speech or translate languages.

## Exam focus

- Sentiment, entities, key phrases, and text insights point to Comprehend.

## Common pitfalls

- Do not choose by product name alone; confirm data type, latency, control, operations, and cost constraints.

## Remember

**Comprehend understands text; Transcribe turns speech into text.**
