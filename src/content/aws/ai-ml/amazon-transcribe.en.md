## In one sentence

An automatic speech-recognition service that converts audio and speech into text.

## Key points

- It fits meeting transcripts, captions, contact-center analysis, and searchable audio.
- The transcript can then be analyzed by Comprehend for sentiment or entities.
- Transcribe does not read text aloud; Polly does that.

## Exam focus

- Choose Transcribe for speech to text, captions, and transcription.

## Common pitfalls

- Do not choose by product name alone; confirm data type, latency, control, operations, and cost constraints.

## Remember

**Transcribe: speech → text.**
