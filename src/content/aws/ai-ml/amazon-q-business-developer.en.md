## In one sentence

Amazon Q Business is an enterprise knowledge assistant; Amazon Q Developer supports software development and AWS workflows.

## Key points

- Q Business connects enterprise sources and must honor identity and content permissions.
- Q Developer assists code understanding, generation, troubleshooting, and AWS development tasks.
- Compare Amazon Bedrock when the goal is to build your own generative-AI product.

## Exam focus

- Choose Q Business for enterprise knowledge and Q Developer for coding and AWS development.

## Common pitfalls

- Do not choose by product name alone; confirm data type, latency, control, operations, and cost constraints.

## Remember

**Business finds enterprise knowledge; Developer helps build; Bedrock lets you build your own.**
