## One-line summary

Analyze the current estate, licensing, and target scenarios to answer whether a migration is worthwhile and what it may cost.

## Core role

- Compare current on-premises cost with modeled AWS cost.
- Identify overprovisioned servers and right-sizing candidates from utilization data.
- Compare licensing approaches such as BYOL and License Included.
- Produce target-state scenarios and a migration business case for decision makers.

## Exam focus and pitfalls

- TCO, potential savings, licensing, and a business case point to Migration Evaluator.
- It belongs to Assess and does not replicate servers.
- Discovery Service focuses on technical assets and dependencies; Pricing Calculator estimates from manually supplied architecture assumptions.
- Validate recommendations against peaks, growth, reliability, and input-data quality.

## Key takeaway

**Discovery maps the estate; Evaluator models cost, licensing, and migration value.**
