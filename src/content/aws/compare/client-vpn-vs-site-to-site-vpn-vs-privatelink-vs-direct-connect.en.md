## In one sentence

> Choose by who connects, whether a whole network is linked, whether access is service-private, and whether a dedicated circuit is required.

## Key points

- Client VPN connects individual remote users.
- Site-to-Site VPN connects networks with encrypted IPsec over the internet.
- PrivateLink and VPC endpoints provide private service access without exposing a full network path.

## Exam takeaway

> Direct Connect provides dedicated private connectivity with consistent performance but is not encrypted by default.
