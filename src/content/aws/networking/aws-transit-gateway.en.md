## In one sentence

> A regional hub that connects many VPCs and on-premises networks through centralized routing.

## Key points

- Transit Gateway reduces the complexity of many point-to-point peering or VPN connections.
- Attachments connect VPCs, VPNs, Direct Connect gateways, and peered transit gateways.
- Transit Gateway route tables support segmentation between groups of networks.

## Exam takeaway

> It is a routed hub, not a firewall; inspection and security controls must be designed separately.
