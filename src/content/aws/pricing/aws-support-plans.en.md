## Current plan line (August 2026)

- **Basic** includes account and billing help and self-service resources, but no technical-case response SLA.
- **Business Support+** targets production workloads with 24/7 experts, broad Trusted Advisor checks, and a 30-minute initial human-engagement target for business-critical incidents.
- **Enterprise Support** adds a designated TAM, proactive reviews, and a 15-minute production-critical response target.
- **Unified Operations** adds deeper, designated, proactive operations for mission-critical workloads.

Developer Support, Business Support, and Enterprise On-Ramp may appear in older material; AWS has announced end of support on January 1, 2027 outside GovCloud (US).

Response times are initial-response targets, not resolution guarantees, and support does not replace resilient architecture.

## Key takeaway

**Production starts with Business Support+; a designated TAM points to Enterprise; mission-critical proactive operations point to Unified Operations.**
