## Pricing principles

- **Pay as you go** reduces upfront purchases and capacity guessing.
- **Save when you commit** exchanges stable one- or three-year usage commitments for discounts.
- **Pay less by using more** applies tiered or volume pricing to selected services.

## Cost-management workflow

- Billing shows current charges and payments; Budgets alerts on actual or forecast thresholds.
- Cost Explorer analyzes historical costs and trends; Pricing Calculator estimates a planned architecture.
- Organizations consolidates multi-account billing, while activated allocation tags assign costs to teams and projects.
- Compute Optimizer recommends resource sizes; Trusted Advisor checks broader best-practice categories.

Optimize continuously: identify drivers, assign ownership, right-size resources, match pricing models to demand, improve storage and network paths, then add budgets and periodic reviews.

## Support and procurement

The current support line is Basic, Business Support+, Enterprise Support, and Unified Operations. Response times are initial-response targets, not resolution guarantees. Marketplace is for third-party products; APN is for partners and delivery expertise.

## Key takeaway

**Estimate first, analyze actual costs, set guardrails, and choose support by workload criticality.**
