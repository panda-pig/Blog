## One-line summary

Find, purchase, subscribe to, and deploy third-party AMIs, containers, SaaS, data, AI/ML products, and professional services; eligible charges can appear on the AWS bill.

Marketplace is an external product market, while Service Catalog is an organization's approved internal catalog and APN is a partner ecosystem. Third-party licensing, security, and support responsibilities still apply.

## Key takeaway

**Buy a third-party product in Marketplace; find delivery expertise through APN.**
