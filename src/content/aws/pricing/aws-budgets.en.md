## One-line summary

Set cost, usage, Savings Plans, or RI targets and notify stakeholders when actual or forecast values cross thresholds.

## Exam focus and pitfalls

- Budgets supports actual and forecast thresholds, filters, email or SNS notifications, and controlled budget actions.
- A budget does not stop overspending resources by default.
- Budgets compares against a target; anomaly detection finds unusual patterns; Cost Explorer supports analysis.

## Key takeaway

**A defined target plus actual or forecast threshold alerts means AWS Budgets.**
