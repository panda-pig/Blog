## One-line summary

Analyze historical cost and usage by service, account, Region, tag, and other dimensions, with trends and forecasts.

## Use cases and pitfalls

- Compare periods, find cost drivers, and allocate costs to accounts, teams, or projects.
- Locate changes in EC2, NAT Gateway, logs, or data-transfer spend before validating an optimization with operational metrics.
- Cost Explorer is not a pre-deployment estimate, a budget-alert tool, or real-time system monitoring.

## Key takeaway

**Historical cost, trends, grouping, and forecasts point to Cost Explorer.**
