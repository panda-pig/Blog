## One-line summary

Review current charges, invoices, payment information, and cost-management entry points in one place.

## Exam focus

- Use Billing for current charge and payment facts.
- Use Cost Explorer for historical analysis, Budgets for thresholds, and Pricing Calculator for pre-deployment estimates.
- Consolidated billing centralizes payment but does not merge member-account resources or identities.

## Key takeaway

**Current bills, invoices, and payments point to AWS Billing and Cost Management.**
