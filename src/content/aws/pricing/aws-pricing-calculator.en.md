## One-line summary

Estimate the cost of a planned workload by entering service configurations, usage, and commitment assumptions before deployment.

## Exam focus

- Compare Regions, instance sizes, architectures, and pricing models.
- Include data transfer, NAT Gateway, logs, backups, requests, cross-AZ or cross-Region traffic, and support.
- An estimate is not a bill or spending ceiling; actual usage and prices determine final charges.

## Key takeaway

**A pre-deployment estimate points to AWS Pricing Calculator.**
