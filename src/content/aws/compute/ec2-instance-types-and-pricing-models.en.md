## In one sentence

> Select EC2 families by resource profile and pricing models by workload stability and interruption tolerance.

## Key points

- General Purpose balances resources; Compute, Memory, Storage, and Accelerated families target specific bottlenecks.
- On-Demand fits short or unpredictable use without commitment.
- Savings Plans and Reserved Instances trade commitment for discounts on stable workloads.

## Exam takeaway

> Spot offers deeper discounts for interruptible, fault-tolerant jobs; Dedicated options address tenancy or licensing needs.
