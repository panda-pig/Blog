## In one sentence

> A simplified bundle of compute, storage, networking, and fixed-price plans for small and predictable workloads.

## Key points

- Lightsail provides an easier experience than assembling EC2, EBS, VPC, and related services separately.
- It fits small websites, blogs, prototypes, learning environments, and simple applications.
- Predictable bundled pricing is a key advantage.

## Exam takeaway
