## In one sentence

> AWS-managed infrastructure installed at a customer site to run selected AWS services with local latency or residency needs.

## Key points

- Outposts extends AWS infrastructure, APIs, and operational models into on-premises locations.
- It serves workloads needing very low local latency, local processing, or specific data-location requirements.
- Customers provide the site, power, cooling, and connectivity; AWS manages the Outposts infrastructure.

## Exam takeaway

> Outposts is hybrid infrastructure, not another AWS Region or a general replacement for the cloud.
