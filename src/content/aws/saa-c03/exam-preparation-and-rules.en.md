## In one sentence

Use the official Exam Guide to find knowledge gaps, validate with scenario questions and hands-on work, and verify booking rules separately.

## Core summary

The exam has 65 questions in 130 minutes. Fifty are scored and fifteen are unscored without identification. The scaled score ranges from 100 to 1000, with 720 required to pass.

## Study points

- Domain weights: security 30%, resilience 26%, performance 24%, and cost optimization 20%.
- A score of 720 is not 72% correct, and domains do not have separate pass marks.
- Unanswered items are wrong and guesses have no extra penalty; answer first, then flag.
- Verify identity, language, time zone, delivery method, accommodations, and payment when booking.

## Common pitfalls

- Keep exam administration separate from technical knowledge.
- Course completion and high practice scores are not an AWS certification.

## Key memory

**Mark hard constraints, reject options that miss prerequisites, then compare viable choices on operations, performance, reliability, and total cost.**
