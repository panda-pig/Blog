## Why I Started Blogging

In this era of information overload, we are surrounded by massive amounts of content every day. Yet truly personal thoughts are becoming increasingly rare. I hope that through this blog, I can force myself to沉淀 my ideas and form more complete understanding.

## What This Blog Will Cover

- **Technical Articles**: Technical problems I encounter at work, learning insights
- **Life Essays**: Daily thoughts, reading notes, movie reflections
- **Tool Recommendations**: Useful software, plugins, workflows

## About Building This Blog

This blog is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and styled with [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com). It is fully static, loads extremely fast, and supports dark mode and full-text search.

If you also want to build a similar blog, feel free to reference my implementation.

## Final Words

Blogging is something that requires persistence. I don't strive for perfection in every post, but for sincere recording. I hope my sharing can be helpful to you.
