A typical business data analysis project can be organized into four phases and eleven steps, with iteration between phases as the questions and available data evolve.

![A typical business data analysis workflow: define goals, understand the business, assess metrics and data, prepare and explore data, communicate findings, pilot changes, evaluate, and monitor.](/images/data-analysis-workflow/workflow.en.svg)

[Open the full workflow diagram](/images/data-analysis-workflow/workflow.en.svg)

## Four phases

- **Understand the business (①–③):** Define the problem and success criteria, understand the business context, agree on metric definitions, and assess existing data.
- **Prepare the data (④–⑥):** Reuse business data and add collection where needed; integrate, clean, validate, and reconcile records with SQL/Python.
- **Analyze and communicate (⑦–⑧):** Explore segments and trends, use statistical methods where appropriate, and visualize and communicate validated conclusions with tools such as Tableau/BI.
- **Improve and evaluate (⑨–⑪):** Design changes and evaluation methods before piloting, run A/B tests where suitable, decide rollout from the results, and continue monitoring and iterating.
